Opinion: Hassan Sheikh's U-turn on the UAE

Hiigsi Media · Nov 19, 2026 · 4 min read

The president who brought the UAE back now calls it a danger to Somalia's sovereignty. Somalis deserve to know what changed.

This is an opinion piece. For the facts of the president's 17 November address and the UAE's reply, read Hassan Sheikh Mohamud accuses the UAE.

This is a clear U-turn, and it is hard to take seriously. In June 2022, a month after taking office, Hassan Sheikh made the UAE his first foreign trip, the first visit by a Somali president in nearly five years. His government also returned the roughly $9 million that Farmajo's government had seized from an Emirati plane in 2018.

The complaints he makes today are almost the same ones Farmajo made in 2018. Back then, Mogadishu accused the UAE of funding its rivals and stirring up opposition in the federal states. Hassan Sheikh was in opposition at the time, and he was among the opposition leaders who publicly defended the UAE against those very charges. When he came to power, he brought the UAE back. Emirati training and pay for Somali army cadets, stopped during the 2018 quarrel, restarted under him. As recently as March 2025, he flew to Abu Dhabi to meet the UAE president.

Even the UAE has seized on this. Its presidential adviser Anwar Gargash answered the speech by pointing to the president's "frequent shifts" in position. When your opponent's best reply is your own record, that is a problem.

His supporters will say that Israel's recognition of Somaliland changed everything, and that a leader should change course when the facts change. That is a fair point. But Somalis deserve a clear answer: why was the same behaviour acceptable in 2022 and a danger to our sovereignty in 2026? Until the president explains that U-turn, many will ask whether this is about protecting Somalia or about politics in the final year of his term.
