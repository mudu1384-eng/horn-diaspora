About Mohamud

I was born in Somalia, grew up in North Eastern Kenya and moved to Canada in 2019. I live in Ottawa, where I study accounting and work full time.

I write about politics and social issues because people in my community deserve news that is clear, fair and easy to read, in English and in Somali. I separate facts from opinion, link my sources and correct my mistakes openly.
