Hassan Sheikh visits Museveni as Ugandan troops start to leave Somalia

Kamjaroon Online · Nov 24, 2026 · 4 min read · News

On Monday, Somalia's President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud made a one-day working visit to Uganda. Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni met him at State House Entebbe. They talked about security, Ugandan soldiers in Somalia, and Somalia's political problems.

Key points

  • When: Monday, 23 November 2026, at State House Entebbe.
  • Second visit: his second trip to Uganda in under a month.
  • Troops: about 4,500 Ugandan soldiers serve in the AU mission. Uganda plans to pull all its forces out by the end of 2027.
  • Politics: Museveni may travel to Mogadishu to help the government and the opposition talk.

Why the visit matters

Uganda has been one of Somalia's most important partners in the fight against Al-Shabaab. Museveni's country was the first to deploy troops under the African Union Mission in Somalia in 2007 and remains one of the largest troop contributors.

That support is now changing. Uganda began withdrawing on 2 November and plans to pull all forces, including about 4,500 under the AU mission, by the end of 2027. This was also the president's second visit in under a month, after a meeting on 26 October. At that earlier meeting, Hassan Sheikh told Museveni he hoped the soldiers would stay longer.

What the leaders said

Museveni said the two leaders discussed efforts to restore peace, strengthen state institutions and achieve lasting stability in Somalia. He also said he had shared Uganda's experience in building stable institutions through national unity, dialogue and democratic processes.

His statement, however, did not disclose any new agreements or decisions on Uganda's troop withdrawal.

Political tension at home

The visit came during a political crisis in Somalia. On 5 November, Somalia's Supreme Court ruled that parliament's mandate expired on 14 April and the president's term on 15 May. It ordered elections within 90 days. The president rejects the ruling, arguing that the amended constitution gives his government a term ending in May 2027.

The opposition is also unhappy. The main opposition alliance, the Somali Future Council, which includes Puntland and Jubaland, boycotted the president's national forum on 14 November.

Uganda may now help settle this dispute. Hassan Sheikh had insisted that any talks be held inside Somalia, so the compromise is for Museveni to travel to Mogadishu to mediate.

Some media reported that Museveni put pressure on Hassan Sheikh to accept Uganda's mediation, but neither government has confirmed these details.

Money for the mission

On the same day, hours after hosting President Hassan Sheikh Mohamud, President Museveni met US Chargé d'Affaires Mikael Cleverley at State House Entebbe. Museveni welcomed the US commitment to fund the UN Support Office in Somalia (UNSOS), which supplies African Union troops, through 2027.

Hassan Sheikh's trips to Uganda

  • Aug 2022: three-day official visit and business forum.
  • Oct 2024: talks with Museveni.
  • Feb 2025: talks at State House Entebbe.
  • Apr 2025: official visit at State Lodge Nakasero.
  • Jul 2026: summit of AU troop-contributing countries in Kampala.
  • Oct 26, 2026: asked for Ugandan troops to stay longer.
  • Nov 23, 2026: security, troop exit and the political crisis.

What to watch next

  • Museveni's planned trip to Mogadishu to mediate.
  • The early February 2027 election deadline set by the Supreme Court.
  • UN approval of funding for the AU mission before 31 December.
